COMMON QUESTIONS

Good to know.

Questions about the service or a specific project? We can clarify the scope before you make a decision.

Do you shoot advertisements with cameras and actors?+

No. Our current offering focuses on digital-first social creatives, posters and AI-assisted promotional reels. We do not offer on-location video shoots, actors or an in-house film crew.

Is the advertising budget included in your fee?+

Meta platform ad spend and service fees should be treated separately in the estimate. We’ll explain the expected breakdown before a campaign starts.

Can I request only posters or AI reels?+

Yes. You can request individual creative assets or combine them with Meta campaign setup and management.

Can you guarantee leads or sales from Meta ads?+

No. Results depend on audience fit, the offer, competition, budget, creative and follow-up process. Campaigns are planned and evaluated without guaranteed outcomes.

Do you manage Facebook and Instagram together?+

Campaign management can include both Facebook and Instagram through Meta’s advertising tools where the chosen objective and placements allow.
