COMMON QUESTIONS

Good to know.

Questions about the service or a specific project? We can clarify the scope before you make a decision.

Can SEO guarantee first place on Google?+

No. Search rankings are determined by search engines and vary over time. We focus on relevant content, sound technical foundations and sustainable improvements rather than rankings promises.

How long does SEO take to work?+

Search engines need time to crawl and evaluate updates. Timing depends on competition, the site’s current condition and the changes made, so results cannot be promised on a fixed schedule.

Can you help if my new site is not indexed yet?+

We can check robots directives, sitemap accessibility, canonical URLs, internal links and Search Console status. Discovery or an indexing request does not guarantee indexing.

Does SEO require a monthly package?+

Some work, such as technical fixes, can be scoped as a project. Competitive content and ongoing optimisation may benefit from recurring reviews.

Is Google Business Profile part of SEO?+

If your business is eligible for a profile, we can discuss consistent business information and local search guidance. We do not create false office addresses or guarantee local pack placement.
