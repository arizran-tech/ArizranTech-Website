COMMON QUESTIONS

Good to know.

Questions about the service or a specific project? We can clarify the scope before you make a decision.

How much does website development cost in Kerala?+

Pricing depends on the number of pages, design requirements, product features and integrations. Share your requirements and we can provide a project-specific estimate.

Can I update my website after it goes live?+

Yes. The right update workflow depends on whether the site is static or includes a content management system. We can discuss options based on how often you need changes.

Do you build online stores and product catalogues?+

Yes. We can create catalogue websites with enquiries or develop e-commerce functionality where checkout and payments are part of the agreed scope.

Will my website be mobile-friendly?+

Responsive behaviour is part of our website design approach. The final layout is checked across different screen widths before launch.

Is SEO included in website development?+

We can implement technical foundations such as titles, descriptions, clean page structure and sitemap files. Ongoing SEO work is a separate service that can be added if needed.
