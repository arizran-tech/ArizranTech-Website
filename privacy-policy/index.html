ARIZRAN TECH / YOUR PRIVACY

Privacy Policy.

A clear explanation of the information we receive, why we use it, and how we handle client accounts and website enquiries.

Effective date: 22 October 2026  ·  Arizran Tech, Kerala, India

ON THIS PAGE About this policy Information we collect How we use it Client project accounts Analytics & cookies Third-party services Security & retention Your rights Contact us

01. About this policy

Arizran Tech is an online-first digital solutions platform based in Kerala, India. We provide website development, web applications, SEO, digital marketing and related services.

This policy explains how we handle information shared by people who visit our website, contact us or work with us on projects.

02. Information we collect

When you contact us through our website, email or WhatsApp, you may share your name, business name, phone number, email address, service requirements and project details, along with anything else you choose to tell us.

Our website enquiry form prepares a WhatsApp message in your browser. It does not store those form details in a website database. We receive the enquiry when you choose to send your message through WhatsApp.

03. How we use your information

We use enquiry details to understand your business needs, respond to questions, discuss services, prepare quotations and communicate about projects.

For confirmed projects, we also use relevant information to deliver the work, coordinate development, set up agreed services and provide support where arranged.

04. Client accounts and project handover

With your permission, we may help create or configure accounts needed for your project, such as a dedicated email address, GitHub, Cloudflare, domain registration or hosting accounts.

Where possible, these accounts are created under the client's ownership and linked to the client's recovery details. When the project is completed, we provide the agreed account details, access information and relevant project documentation.

We share handover instructions through the client's designated email address and may use a separate secure method for sensitive credentials. Any ongoing access or support after handover depends on what we agree with the client.

05. Website analytics and cookies

We use Google Analytics to understand website visits, popular pages, traffic sources and interactions such as clicks on WhatsApp enquiry buttons. It may process device or browser information and approximate location.

Our website also uses browser storage to remember your preferred light or dark theme. You can manage cookies using your browser settings. We do not intentionally include enquiry names, phone numbers, email addresses or message contents in our analytics events.

06. Third-party services

We use services such as Cloudflare, Google Analytics and WhatsApp to operate the website, understand traffic and communicate with customers. For client projects, we may also use GitHub, domain registrars and hosting providers.

These providers have their own privacy policies. Depending on the service, information may be processed outside India. We share information as needed for the service requested or the operation of our website.

07. Security and information retention

We take reasonable steps to protect client and enquiry information and restrict access to what is needed for project work. No online service can guarantee complete security.

We keep enquiries, project communications and relevant records only as long as reasonably needed for business, support or legal purposes, and review information that is no longer needed.

08. Your questions and privacy rights

You can contact us to ask about information you have shared, request corrections or deletion, or raise privacy concerns. We will review these requests in accordance with applicable requirements.

Our website is primarily intended for businesses and people seeking digital services; it is not specifically directed at children.

09. Updates to this policy

We may update this page when our services, website features or privacy practices change. The effective date shown above will be updated when we publish a revised version.
